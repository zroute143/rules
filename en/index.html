📜 Server Rules

Current version of laws and regulations

1. Attacks

1.1. Attacking Local Players

It is forbidden to attack local (server) players who are in alliances without a valid reason, except for players with the nickname Commander…
  • Players who are not part of an alliance may be considered a farm and attacked for resources.

1.2. Incident Resolution and Diplomacy

If there is a reason for an attack (robbery, assault, insult, provocation, etc.): the player — the person being provoked/insulted/robbed/attacked, etc. — must first of all try to resolve everything through dialogue.
  • If it is not possible to find common ground/compromises, the player submits an appeal up the hierarchy (contacts their R4/R5) — who then contact the representatives of the offending alliance (R4/R5) and likewise try to find common ground/compromises between the alliances in an effort to reach a fair resolution of the situation, without misunderstandings, quarrels, wars, etc.
  • In cases where R4/R5 cannot find a solution, R5 submits an appeal through the President to the TOP 10 council. The President is obliged to raise the matter in the TOP 10 to resolve the conflict. Time allowed for resolution: 24h from the moment the President is contacted.

1.3. Ways to Prevent Conflicts

Solutions for preventing conflicts may include: a duel, retaliatory robbery, an apology and/or compensation (depending on the offense and its severity).
  • If a low-level player is unable to duel or strike back at the offender as compensation, the player has the right to ask a stronger player for help. The one asked is obliged to respond at their own discretion.
  • The offender, by decision of the council or by agreement on accepting punishment, has the right to remove their garrison (in accordance with the decisions of both sides).
  • Punishment: 3 hits on the base of the guilty party without collecting resources for each 1 attack. If the attack is not cancelled and the player continues hitting bases and does not react to the 3 hits — burning to zero.
  • If a player burns a base to zero without reason — burning to zero in response.
  • Leadership Response: Depending on the severity of the rule violation, R4, R5 and council representatives, when contacted, are obliged to immediately respond to the offender and stop the violation.
  • If a player is inadequate, i.e. causing harm to the server, the player is added to the "BL" (Blacklist) for 1 month or until a public apology is made.
  • If the offender disagrees with the punishment or the President's decision: the offender has the right to file an appeal for a re-examination of the offense case and contest it within 6 hours after the council's decision. The council undertakes to consider the situation from both sides and issue exclusively fair decisions regarding offenders.

1.4. Reconnaissance and Cancelled Attacks

A single reconnaissance and/or a single cancelled attack is not an act of offense or a provocative action.
  • Offense: Repeated and/or frequent reconnaissance, simultaneous reconnaissance on other alliance members, as well as frequent cancelled attacks on one or more players from the same alliance — is regarded as provocative/inadequate behavior and constitutes an offense.
2. Robberies

2.1. Prohibition of Robbery and Rules for Accidental Actions

It is forbidden to rob transport, special operations, and personal farms of local (server) players.
  • In case of accidental robbery, you should contact the player who was robbed and resolve the issue peacefully to avoid further conflict (see 1.3). Waiting time for resolution: 12h.
  • An accidental robbery is an unintentional "click" on a special operation on Saturdays (at a moment when an attack on the enemy is underway and quick action is required regarding the attack on the enemy on Saturdays).
  • A screenshot or video showing that the enemy's castle is next to the special operation that was robbed can serve as evidence.
  • Punishment: If a decision is made to punish the player — 1 hit without collecting.
3. Tiles

3.1. Prohibition of Knocking Players Off Resource Mines

It is forbidden to knock a player off a tile (mine) that is occupied by another player.
  • Punishment: 3 hits without collecting.
4. Speech

4.1. Offensive Statements

It is forbidden to use offensive expressions toward anyone for provocation, self-assertion, etc. (be restrained, treat each other with mutual understanding and respect).
  • 4.2. It is forbidden to stir up political disputes or resort to personal attacks (do not bring the real world into the game. Want to discuss politics with someone? Go to private messages).
  • 4.3. It is strictly forbidden to offensively touch upon family, religion, race, creed, etc., regarding yourself and/or other players.
  • Punishment: For gross insults and touching upon the listed topics, the player is declared outlawed.
  • A player who violates this rule is added to the "BL" list and is not protected by the server rules and/or any alliance. Consequently: anyone has the right to attack them until they make a public and sincere apology.
  • If a player who violated this rule joins an alliance that actively defends them — the representatives of that alliance (R4, R5) are automatically added to the "BL" list and may be attacked by any server user until they expel the offender from the alliance (for prompt decision-making).
  • It is forbidden for R5 and R4 to give coordinates in the server's general chat — this is a provocation for active actions. In case of violation, the issue must be resolved directly and based on the outcome of events.
  • If the culprit shared coordinates and nothing happened, the issue is resolved diplomatically through conversation. If it led to an attack by anyone — 3 hits on the base without collecting.
5. Alliances

5.1 – 5.2. Responsibility of Leaders and the "Expedition Madness" Event

When creating an alliance, its leader is the responsible person for every member of their alliance and must be aware of all the hardships and cares that await them.
  • The alliance leader is obliged to cooperate with other alliances (in case other alliances reach out regarding violations, respond immediately and give feedback).
  • They are subject to mandatory participation in the struggle regarding the "Expedition Madness" event (at minimum, try to rally people and bring points to the server even without participating in the battle).
  • If alliance representatives refuse to cooperate and resolve the issue by seeking compromises, a complaint is filed and sent to the TOP 10 council, which then issues a fair decision regarding the representatives of that alliance (average waiting time for alliance representatives' inaction: 24 hours from the moment contact is attempted).

5.3. Alliance Leader

The alliance leader has the full right to seek help from other alliances (for example, for advice, or to conclude unions/world and/or mutually beneficial agreements with other alliances).

5.4. Infringement of the Rights of Small Alliances

It is forbidden to infringe upon the rights and powers of, or create threats from a strong alliance, as well as to create obstacles to the development of small alliances (Example: "Become our academy or we will burn you").

5.5. Falsification of the Rating

It is forbidden to falsify the rating by poaching players from allied alliances.
  • Punishment: In case of violation of this rule, the person — i.e. the player from whom the violation originates — gives up leadership, and the rating is not counted.
6. Cities

6.1. Distribution and City Limits

Level 1 cities are active for actions. Cities 2–6 are distributed by agreement among the TOP 10. It is also not forbidden to transfer cities at your own discretion without taking an extra city.
  • Limit: An alliance is entitled to 6 cities. If an alliance decides to gift a city, it retains 5 cities. It cannot take an extra city.

6.2. Rules for Transferring Level 6 Cities

Level 6 cities are transferred clockwise every week on Friday at 15:00 (the TOP 3 may agree on the time in advance).
  • Violation: If this rule is violated, all combat actions cease for 24 hours.
  • The alliance affected by the violation files an appeal to the council, where the council, at its discretion (mandatorily reviewing evidence from both sides and relying on the rulebook), issues a fair decision to prevent conflicts.
  • Punishment: Up to and including transferring the city to the attacking alliance, if the violation came from the alliance defending the city.
  • An alliance that violated the rule is forbidden to conduct combat actions against that city again for a period of 7 days.

6.3. Rating Snapshot and Distribution

The rating is recorded daily at 19:00 MSK and sent to the Lolka leaders chat. Whichever alliance was more often higher in the rating during the week takes the corresponding TOP city.
  • Exception: Saturday — due to the VS and Madness events, the snapshot is taken at 16:00 and 19:00.
  • In case of disputes, the difference of 100 million BM is used as the deciding factor.
7. Prohibition of Sedition

7.1. Contracted Attacks and Cross-Server Hiring

It is forbidden to stir up sedition on the server, to hire players to burn others from alliances on another server.
  • Punishment: Unconditional exclusion from the NAP.
✦ Important Notes and Terms

General Provisions and Server Concepts

Every player on the server is subject to the rules, without any exceptions.
  • Before adopting rules or making any changes to their structure, the decision is made by a council of the TOP 10 alliances of the server.
  • Tiles — resource mines on the global map.
  • Buff — a % bonus from the Capitol/city/skill minister.
  • BL (Blacklist): Players on it are players who are on the verge of being OUTLAWED, but have a 7-day period to fix the situation. After 7 days, a player who has not improved their position automatically becomes outlawed.
👑 President, Entourage, Vice President, Capitol Struggle, Rewards, and Council

1. Election and Powers of the President

The President is the primary representative figure at the level of other server representatives and undertakes to be fair and to strive for the well-being of their server.
  • 1.1. Election: The President is chosen by a decision of a council of several alliances, or follows the established pattern among the TOP 3 of passing the Capitol from hand to hand among the TOP 3 alliances.
  • 1.2. Rights: Has the full right to conclude unions with other servers, as well as to appoint a Vice President, issue presidential rewards, vote, and — with the approval of a council of 5 — may introduce amendments to the rules.
  • 1.3. Removal: If the President is inactive and/or inadequate, or violates server rules — all such matters are brought before the council, after which the President is removed from office for an indefinite period. Their place is taken by a person from their entourage.

2. President's Entourage

To ease their duties, the President has the full right to create an entourage of 3 people who are obliged to help both the President and the council (keep order, make decisions, develop tactics for the Capitol struggle, and much more).
  • Equality before the law: The entourage is nothing special and has no privileges in the form of exceptions to any of the server rules, and bears responsibility just like ordinary players (the same applies to the President and the council).

3. Vice President

A Vice President is a specific person appointed who, at the request of players, simply moves them up the queue for ministers.
  • As such, they have no major assignments.

4. Capitol, Capitol Struggle, and Rewards

4.1. The Capitol is one of the important structures for the server, where the President is the representative figure of the server. The Capitol stores rewards issued by the President, as well as the ability to take a minister position to use a buff for something related to the development of your castle.
  • Any server user has the right to take a minister position, regardless of castle level or contribution to the server.

4.2. Regarding the struggle for the Capitol, every alliance is obliged to fight to the best of its abilities.
  • The struggle is not only the battle itself but also bringing points to the server throughout the entire week during which the "Expedition Madness" event takes place (winning Pandora, robbing the needed transport, winning VS, dealing damage, and much more). The President, in turn, undertakes to support and rally players by publishing information about this event through presidential mail.
  • In case of victory: If the enemy server is defeated, the local server receives gifts in the form of envelopes with diamonds, diamond mines, as well as 2 additional Capitol minister positions that have slightly more buffs for the development of your castle.
  • In case of defeat: In case of defeat, your server loses nothing and retains the minister positions of its own Capitol.
